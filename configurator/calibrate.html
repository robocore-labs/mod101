Servos

no bus

One card per servo found on the bus, and one joint per servo. The mapping is this page's own — there is no config stored on the arm — and the defaults follow mod101_description/urdf/mod101_macro.xacro. ROS joint is picked from the joints the loaded URDF actually declares, which is what the model tab checks against. Sign flips a joint whose servo counts opposite to its URDF axis; joint_wrist_tilt is 0 -1 0, hence −1 by default. Edits persist locally and travel with the exported JSON.

Jogging is raw bus control, independent of any calibration: − and + command one step from where the servo is, over the full 0…4095 encoder range and not a calibrated travel limit. One press is one step and nothing repeats while held, because on an assembled arm the way into a hard stop is a control that keeps going — but the steps do add up, so watch the joint and keep the disarm button in reach.

nothing calibrated

Turns the calibrated tick ranges into src/mod101_control/config/calibration.yaml (ROS, radians relative to each joint's home) and a LeRobot calibration JSON. STS3215 encoders are 4096 ticks/rev and direct-drive, so ticks convert straight to radians — the same counts serve both stacks.
