Servo setup

no bus

One row per servo found on the bus. The mapping is this page's own — there is no config stored on the arm — and the defaults follow mod101_description/urdf/mod101_macro.xacro. Mirror pairs a second servo to the same joint (0 = single). Sign flips a joint whose servo counts opposite to its URDF axis; joint_wrist_tilt is 0 -1 0, hence −1 by default. Edits persist locally and travel with the exported JSON — press Rebuild joints after changing a mirror.
