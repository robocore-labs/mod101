  All math is first-order static balance at the bottleneck (binding) pitch joint:
  the two pitch servos hold the arm's self-weight plus payload at the stated
  reach. Regimes: Duty-cycle (70 % of stall) is the rated continuous
  headline — the manufacturer's safe-load figure, not a thermal limit; Holding
  (50 %) is a conservative static hold; Peak (100 %) is momentary stall only.
  Real torque drops with temperature, voltage, and duty — verify on the
  bench. Negative payload means the arm can't hold its own weight at that
  length — shorten the rail or fit a bigger motor.
