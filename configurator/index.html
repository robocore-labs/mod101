Numbers assume the shoulder pitch joint is the bottleneck (lowest leverage). Arm self-weight torque is modelled as the actuated mass at one-third of the current reach; payload contributes torque at full reach. Negative numbers mean the arm can't even hold its own weight at this length — pick a shorter extrusion.
